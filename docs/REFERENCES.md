# Conceptual References & Literature

Lookahead is written from scratch. All algorithms and designs are built from published chess programming literature, open academic papers, and computer chess theory.

---

## Foundational Papers & Articles

1. **Alpha-Beta Pruning**:
   * Knuth, Donald E., and Ronald W. Moore. *"An analysis of alpha-beta pruning."* Artificial Intelligence 6.4 (1975): 293-326.
2. **Quiescence Search & Horizon Effect**:
   * Shannon, Claude E. *"Programming a computer for playing chess."* The London, Edinburgh, and Dublin Philosophical Magazine and Journal of Science 41.314 (1950): 256-275.
3. **Zobrist Hashing**:
   * Zobrist, Albert L. *"A new hashing method with applications for game playing."* Technical Report 88, Computer Sciences Department, University of Wisconsin, Madison (1970).
4. **Magic Bitboards**:
   * Pradu, Fabien. *"Magic Bitboards."* Chess Programming Wiki (2007).
5. **Null Move Heuristic**:
   * Donninger, Chrilly. *"Null Move and Deep Search: Clear-Sky Thinking."* ICCA Journal, Vol. 16, No. 3 (1993).
6. **Late Move Reductions (LMR)**:
   * Heinz, Ernst A. *"Scalable Search in Computer Chess."* Vieweg+Teubner Verlag (2000).
